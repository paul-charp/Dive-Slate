# Dive Slate 0.4.4 — the preview shows the size you are actually choosing

Picking a layout now changes the slate and nothing else.

Until this release the preview sized itself to whatever it was drawing: every
layout was scaled up to the same width, and the frame around it grew and shrank
to fit. Wide, Tall, Compact and Watch therefore all arrived on screen at roughly
the same size — which is the one difference between them that matters. Watch is
a badge for the corner of a shot; it takes about a third of the frame's width,
and there was nowhere in the app you could see that before you saved the PNG.

Now the checkerboard is a fixed frame standing in for the footage, and each
layout draws at its own share of it: Wide and Tall span it, Compact takes about
36%, Watch about 32%. Nothing under the preview moves when you switch, so the
layout chips stay under your finger.

The exported PNG is unchanged. This was always what the file contained — only
the preview was telling you otherwise.

## Also in this release

**The panel starts at 85%.** It used to open at each palette's computed minimum,
which is the least panel that still clears the contrast ratio against the worst
possible backdrop. That is the right floor and it still holds the slider, but it
is a floor, and video is not a worst-case still frame. Opening higher puts the
legible version in front of you first and lets you take it down from there.

**The checkerboard is mid-grey rather than near-white.** The old greys were the
white worst case the contrast floors are computed against — useful as a test,
misleading as a preview, because nobody drops a slate onto white. The palettes
for dark footage now sit on something closer to what they will meet.
