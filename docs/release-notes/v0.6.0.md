# Dive Slate 0.6.0 — it follows your phone now, and the profile is a curve

The app was dark whatever your phone was set to. It follows the system setting
now, light or dark, and still takes its accent from your wallpaper on Android 12
and up.

What made that safe to change is the thing that *doesn't* follow it: the
checkerboard behind the preview. That square isn't decoration, it stands in for
the footage your slate is going to land on — so it stays the same mid-grey in
both modes, and a palette you judge on a light phone looks the same on the video
as one you judged on a dark phone. Only the frame around it moves.

None of this reaches the drawing. The slate's own dark/light palettes are a
statement about your footage and are still yours to pick.

## The profile is drawn as a curve

A dive profile is a curve in the water; the jagged line was the sampling showing
through. Every style but **Dive computer** now draws it smoothed, and **Smooth
curve** under Elements turns that off when you want every tooth of a sawtooth
bottom rather than a line through them.

Dive computer doesn't offer it, because its segment screen quantises the profile
to one minute and one metre and a curve through a staircase is just a staircase
with rounded corners.

The smoothing can't lie about the dive. It changes how the line travels between
two depths and never invents a third: the deepest point of your dive is on the
drawing whatever the setting, so the picture can't drift away from the number
printed beside it.

## Easier to get around

**The style picker is one row instead of four.** Eight styles as a wrapping grid
of chips pushed the preview off the top of the screen before you'd touched
anything. It's a menu now, and the preview stays where you can see it — which
matters, because the preview is the only thing that really tells you what a
style looks like.

**The palettes are one row instead of two.** Dark ones first, then light. The
swatches are drawn on the surface they were checked against, so the grouping was
always visible without the two headings saying it.

**The arrows for stepping through a batch are visible.** They were two thin grey
chevrons under the busiest part of the screen — the one control a multi-dive
export can't be used without, drawn as the faintest thing near it. They now sit
in filled buttons with the position between them.

**Update notices close with a cross** rather than "Not now", which only ever
made sense for one of the four things that banner says. And the start screen
links to the project on GitHub — there's no store page, so that's the only place
saying what changed.

## Fixed

**Backing out of a multi-dive edit no longer loses your selection.** It never
actually lost it — the rows kept their highlight while every control claimed
nothing was selected, and tapping Select brought all three back as though from
nowhere. Backing out now lands you exactly where you left.

**The Watch badge sets proper numerals in every style.** One style scaled its
figures down to pay for its own padding, which left the smallest slate setting
the *smallest* numbers — smaller than the layout that spans the whole frame, and
smaller than Compact on a bigger badge. The Watch layout stacks its figures
precisely so they can be large, and now they are.

## The Material style has been removed

It's gone, along with its eight palettes, so there are eight styles rather than
nine. If it was the one you used, **Wrapped** is the nearest thing still here: a
single opaque card with the figures on it.

Wrapped has also lost its corner sparkles.
