# Dive Slate 0.5.0 — eight more ways to draw the same dive

There was one look. Flat, geometric, reading as instrumentation; the only thing
you could change about it was the colour.

There are nine now, and they are different pictures rather than recolourings of
one:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Modern** | flat and geometric — still the default, unchanged |
| **Wrapped** | a loud opaque card, made to be posted |
| **Sticker** | rounded and ringed, the profile a warm-to-cool ramp |
| **Magazine** | editorial type straight on the footage, no card at all |
| **Frosted** | a pane of glass with a lit edge |
| **HUD** | a cut-cornered panel and a trace that glows |
| **Dive computer** | a bezel, a segment screen, the profile drawn as steps |
| **Survey** | grained paper with a labelled depth grid |
| **Material** | a tonal card with each figure in its own chip |

Every one of them works with every layout, so Wide, Tall, Compact and Watch mean
exactly what they did: the style decides how the marks are drawn, the layout
decides where they go.

Each style brings its own palettes — thirty-two in all — and each offers at
least one for dark footage and one for a pale background, so switching style
keeps whichever you were on. They are still computed and machine-checked rather
than picked by eye, and a palette that fails still stops the build instead of
reaching your screen.

## Gas switches read properly now

A marker on the profile, a dashed leader, and the mix named in a tab on the
surface line. The name used to sit beside the marker, which meant it landed
wherever the diver happened to be — on the curve, inside the hatched ceiling, or
off the edge of the frame near the end of a dive.

## Several dives at once

Select any number of dives from a log and get one slate each, from one pass over
the settings.

The parts worth knowing are the ones that stop a batch hiding something. A dive
with no depth samples is **refused in the list**, with the reason on its row,
rather than selected and dropped at the end. A hand-picked figure the other
dives lack is **counted before you export** — "GF is missing on 3 of 6" — rather
than quietly absent from some of the images. And a run that could not finish
says `Saved 11 of 12` and why, never just "done".

Open more than one file and the list groups by file rather than merging them.
Two exports overlapping is an ordinary accident, and merged they become rows
that look identical with no honest way to tell them apart.

## Also in this release

**Exporting no longer stalls the app.** Rendering and compressing happen off the
main thread, one image at a time. A slate at export scale is about thirty
megabytes of bitmap; twenty of those at once is not four times faster, it is a
crash.

**A batch shares one timestamp**, then sorts by dive number, so it lands
together in the gallery.
