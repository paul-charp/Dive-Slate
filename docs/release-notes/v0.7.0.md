# Dive Slate 0.7.0 — recent dives, saved looks, feet

## New

- **Recent dives** on the start screen. Tap one to reopen it, in the look you
  left it in. Twenty dives, oldest dropped first.
- **Clear**, on the recent list, with the size the cached logs occupy beside it.
- **Feet and Fahrenheit.** Depth, temperature, gas in cubic feet, and a depth
  grid labelled in feet rather than converted metres.
- **Save as default**, **Restore default**, and a confirmed **Factory reset**.

## Changed

- The defaults moved into the top bar's overflow menu.
- Units sit on the Figures heading line, and the m/ft control is smaller.
- The checkerboard toggle is drawn on the preview itself.
- The sample dive is offered only until you have recent dives of your own.

## Fixed

- Rotating the phone no longer loses the open log, the chosen look, an export in
  progress, or where you were in a batch.
- A shared file no longer lists every dive twice after a rotation.
- A cancelled export no longer leaves the progress bar stuck part way.
- **Smooth curve** no longer appears on **Dive computer**, where pressing it did
  nothing.
