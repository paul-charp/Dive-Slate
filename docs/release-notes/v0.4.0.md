# Dive Slate 0.4.0 — badges for the corner of a shot

The slate used to come in two shapes, and both of them spanned the frame. This
release adds two that do not: small badges meant to sit in a corner of footage
that is doing its own talking.

## Two new layouts

**Compact** — 460 × 388. Depth and runtime first, side by side, with the profile
cut to a strip beneath them. A little over a third of the width of a 1080 frame.

**Watch** — 400 × 459, and very nearly square. The two figures are stacked, one
above the other, so each gets the badge's full width instead of half of it —
which is how a badge a third of the frame's width ends up with numerals *larger*
than the layout that spans all of it. The profile becomes a sparkline: the shape
that tells you which dive it was, not something you read depths off.

Both put the numbers before the profile, which is the other half of what makes
them work at that size. A depth curve at badge scale is a texture; the figures
are what anyone actually reads.

## Wide and Tall carry four figures

They were held to three, which had nothing to do with what they can fit. Four is
what they hold without a figure being typeset smaller than the rest — Tall runs
out of room before Wide does, despite being the same width, because its numerals
are half again as large.

Each layout now states its own limit, and the picker shows it: the figure chips
grey out once you have spent the budget, and switching to a narrower layout trims
your choice in front of you. Nothing is dropped quietly at the point of drawing —
a figure silently missing looks exactly like a log that never recorded one.

## Long names and long values fit

A site called `SS Thistlegorm, Sha'ab Ali` needs more width than a 400px badge
has, and a trimix dive prints its gases as `Tx18/45, EAN50, O2`, which is four
times the length of any number on the slate. Both are now fitted to the space
they have rather than being left to run into whatever is beside them.

## Install

Download **`dive-slate-0.4.0.apk`** below and open it. **Android 10 or newer.**
`dive-slate-0.4.0.apk.sha256` holds its checksum if you want to verify the
download by hand.

If you have 0.3.0 installed, the app will have offered you this one already, or
you can install this APK straight over it — same signing key, so it updates in
place and keeps your settings.
